# DPA AD pricing plan

## Why $99 flat can't get you there
At $99/month each customer is worth **$1,188 a year**, so $100M ARR needs **~84,000 paying brands** at once. With typical small-business churn of 3–5% a month, you'd also need 30–40k new brands every year just to stay level. A flat price also charges a 25-product store the same as a 40,000-product retailer.

## Value metric: active products, plus video renders
- Grows as the brand grows: more products means more ad spend and more value.
- Easy to understand: "How many products do you have?"
- Video is your highest cost, so it's metered separately.
- Ad platforms (Meta, TikTok, Google, Pinterest, Snap) set the tier, not usage.

## Trial
14 days free, no card, on the brand's own catalog (up to 100 products, watermarked video). No free-forever plan: it costs render money and attracts brands under 20 products.
